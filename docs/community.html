Servers

Shared Subsonic & Navidrome servers

Servers their owners opened to everyone. Found one you like? Add to Viboplr opens the app with the connection filled in — no typing.

These are servers people chose to share publicly. Listings carry shared logins in plain text, filled into the app when you add one. Treat them as public — never list a private server, and don't reuse a password you use elsewhere.

Cue sheets

Cards and clips for the songs you love

Timed cards and lyric clips that play over Now Playing, written by listeners and their AI assistants. A sheet follows the song, so it plays over any copy of it you have. Importing one takes the Viboplr Community plugin plugin, arriving with the next Viboplr release.

Share your own

Sign in with GitHub on community.viboplr.com — no password to create. You manage only what you share.

1

List a server

Give it a name, the address, a short description, tags, and the shared login you're comfortable making public. Everyone gets a one-click Add to Viboplr.

2

Publish a cue sheet

Made a sheet for a song — yourself or with your AI assistant? Publish it from the Community plugin's My sheets tab, or right-click the track.

3

Keep it tidy

Edit or remove anything you've shared from your account. Spot a problem in someone else's? Report it there.
