Community

Vibo Community is where Vibo listeners share what makes their music better: cue sheets, synced lyrics, mixtapes and comments. It lives on its own site.

Go to community.viboplr.com

What you'll find there

Music

Look up any song, album or artist to see what listeners shared about it: cue sheets for long mixes, synced lyrics, likes and comments.

Mixtapes

Playlists people put together, ready to open in Vibo and play from your own library.

Subsonic servers

Subsonic / Navidrome servers that members open to others. Only signed-in members can see them.

Taking part

Browsing needs no account. To share, sign in with GitHub on community.viboplr.com; there's no password to create. Inside Vibo, Community comes through the free Vibo Community plugin plugin, which opens what you find there in the app and shares what you make. Find it in Vibo under Extensions → Plugins.
