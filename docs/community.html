Community

Vibo Community is a separate site, community.viboplr.com, where listeners share cue sheets, synced lyrics, mixtapes and comments.

Music

Look up a song, album or artist to see what people shared about it.

Mixtapes

Playlists other listeners made, which open in Vibo and play from your own library.

Subsonic servers

Subsonic and Navidrome servers that members share with each other. Only signed-in members can see them.

Taking part

Browsing needs no account. To share, sign in with GitHub on the community site. Inside Vibo, the free Vibo Community pluginplugin opens what you find there and shares what you make; it is under Extensions → Plugins.
