For collectors & curators

Your collection,
kept your way

Years of rips, downloads and purchases deserve better than “Unknown Artist”. Viboplr gives you the tools to fix a big library properly — in the files themselves, not just in the app.

Bulk edit

Fix a hundred tracks at once

Select any number of tracks and set the artist, album artist, album, year or tags in one go. Fields that differ across the selection say so, so you never overwrite something by accident. The changes are written into your audio files’ tags, so every other app sees them too.

Tags in Replace, Add or Remove mode — written to the files’ genre
Title and track number for single tracks
Tag suggestions from your library and from Last.fm’s community tagsplugin
Screenshot: bulk edit A whole album selected, “Multiple values” on one field, community tag suggestions open
Album artists

Compilations, done right

Viboplr files an album under its album artist. A tagged compilation becomes one album owned by “Various Artists”, while every track keeps its own performer — no more soundtrack split into twenty one-song albums.

Untagged compilation? Set its album artist in bulk edit and it merges
Full rescan picks up tag edits made in other apps
Collections refresh on a schedule you choose
Upgrades

Better copy? Swap it in place

Found a lossless version of a track you only had as an MP3? Viboplr downloads it, shows you the old and new file side by side — format, size, path — and replaces the file only when you confirm. The track stays in your playlists and keeps its likes.

Compare before you replace — nothing changes until you say so
Works with your download plugins, like Soulseekplugin

Big libraries

Every format, found instantly

FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, APE, WavPack, Musepack, DSD, Opus, Ogg, MP3, AAC and more — plus your music videos. A full-text search index finds any artist, album or track as you type, and it doesn’t care about accents or case: “bjork” finds Björk.

Local folders and Subsonic/Navidrome servers in one library
Artwork from your folders, embedded tags, or providers like MusicBrainz and Deezerplugin
