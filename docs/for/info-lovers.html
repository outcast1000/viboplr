For music lovers who love info

Every song
has a story

Who wrote it, what the lyrics mean, what the critics said, who sounds like them. Viboplr puts it all next to the music, so you never have to leave the song to learn about it.

Lyrics

Sing along, line by line

Synced lyrics light up as the song plays, karaoke-style. Tap any line to jump there. If a file’s timing is a little off, nudge it and Viboplr remembers the fix for that track.

Your own lyrics first: embedded in the file, a .lrc beside it, or a Lyrics folder
Then online sources such as LRCLIB and Lyrics.ovhplugin
Search every lyric you’ve seen to find “that song with the line about…”plugin

Artist pages

Know who you’re hearing

Open any artist for their biography, listener stats, similar artists and top songs. Top songs you don’t own yet appear right in the track list, marked “Not in library” — play them anyway, or start a radio from them.

Album pages

See the whole album

An album page shows the full tracklist, not just the songs you happen to have, with the ones you’re missing slotted into place. Read its review, see which tracks are the fan favourites, and open albums you don’t own at all.
