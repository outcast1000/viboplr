For rediscoverers

You own thousands of songs.

Viboplr’s home screen and radio are built from your own library and your own listening, so the albums you forgot about find their way back to you.

Home

Shelves fill themselves from what you own and how you listen, and refresh every day. Reorder them, hide the ones you don’t want, and switch on more.

Radio

A station from any song

Right-click any track, or hit Radio on an artist, album or genre page, and Viboplr builds a station: the seed, more from that artist, and artists who share its sound — all from your library. The home carousel mixes stations you’ll recognise with ones you’ve never played.

Tune it: how much of the seed artist, favorites or discovery, spread across artists
Works even from a song you don’t own, if you have the artist
Songs you’ve disliked never come back
Mixes

Fresh mixes, every day

Every 24 hours Viboplr writes new playlists from your listening: mixes around your most-played artists and genres, decade mixes, and a Discovery mix of liked songs you’ve drifted away from.

Auto-continue

The music never stops

When the queue runs out, auto-continue keeps going with something that fits: the same artist, the same genre, your most played, your likes, or a wild card. You set the balance.
